Our Story

WE ARE
THE ENERGY

Motion Tees wasn't born in a boardroom — it was born in the streets. We believe style is a living, breathing thing: constantly shifting, constantly moving. Our pieces are designed for those who refuse to stay static.

Whether it's the weight of our premium cotton or the story behind every print, everything we create is a tribute to the hustle, the flow, and the movement of Kenyan culture.

We commission every design from a local Kenyan artist, paying a 15% royalty on every unit sold. When you buy from us, you're buying from them.

Artist Programme

WE INVEST IN
OUR CREATORS

Every design on a Motion Tees garment is commissioned from a Kenyan artist. They're not freelancers-for-hire — they're equity partners in every drop. For each unit sold, 15% of the sale price goes directly to the artist who created the design. No one-off payments. No buyouts. Just fair, ongoing earnings from their work.

Westlands, Nairobi
Specialises in urban street art and bold typographic designs. Known for his "Nairobi Hustle" series that celebrates city life and grind culture. Has collaborated with 3 NGOs and 2 fashion houses before joining Motion Tees.

Step 01
ARTIST COMMISSIONS DESIGN
We brief artists on each seasonal drop theme. They create the original artwork — full creative control, no corporate interference.
Step 02
WE PRODUCE & SELL
Motion Tees handles production, quality control, e-commerce, and delivery. Artists focus on what they do best — creating.
Step 03
15% PAID EVERY MONTH
Every month, artists receive a transparent royalty statement and payment via M-Pesa for 15% of all units sold bearing their design.
